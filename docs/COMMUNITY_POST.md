## OpenOMSI SimHub Bridge

I have published an unofficial community bridge that connects openOMSI telemetry to SimHub on Windows. It can drive dashboards, Arduino devices, RGB matrix displays, Shake-It motors and bass shakers.

Features include speed, brake, throttle, engine data, doors, lights, turn indicators, D/N/R for automatic and electric buses, manual gears and an RGB matrix helper.
